## Title

Pantry Relay: From Promised Food to a Confirmed Receipt | Hack Away Hunger

## Description

One pantry needs produce for tomorrow. Another has food it can spare. Pantry Relay helps an approved network connect the two, protect each pantry's own reserve, and record what the receiver actually accepted.

In this fictional demonstration, 120 pounds are dispatched and 112 pounds are accepted. The report counts 112, keeps the 8-pound exception and leaves the remaining gap visible.

Project lead: Shivam Gupta
Built for: Hack Away Hunger 2026
Try the app: https://pantryrelay.web.app
Source code: https://github.com/shi1720/Hack-Away-Hunger
Testing instructions: https://github.com/shi1720/Hack-Away-Hunger/blob/main/submission/TESTING-INSTRUCTIONS.md

The working release includes accounts, isolated networks, role permissions, an explainable planner, protected reserves, pickup and receipt records, failed-delivery recovery, audit history and CSV exports. It requires no paid AI or mapping API to operate.

The next step is a supervised pilot with one network coordinator and three nearby pantries. The proposed $149 monthly network price is an unvalidated hypothesis. No customer, nonprofit partnership, field impact or meals-served claim is implied.

All pantry names, food records and results shown are fictional sample data. This video uses synthetic narration, not a recording of Shivam's voice. AI tools assisted extensively with research, development, testing and documentation; Shivam directed the project and its priorities.
